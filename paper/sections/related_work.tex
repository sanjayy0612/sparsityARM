\section{Related Work}

DejaVu introduced input-dependent predictors for contextual sparsity in
transformer MLP and attention components~\cite{dejavu}. LLM in a Flash showed
that storage-aware inference benefits from loading larger contiguous bundled
rows and columns rather than many fragmented pieces~\cite{llmflash}. That
observation motivates ARM-Sparse's layout hypothesis, although our weights
remain in RAM and our objective is CPU latency rather than model capacity.

PowerInfer uses hot/cold neuron locality to divide sparse inference work across
consumer CPU and GPU resources~\cite{powerinfer}. PowerInfer-2 elevates neuron
clusters to scheduling and storage units on smartphones~\cite{powerinfer2}, so
clustered execution itself is prior art. Dynamic Input Pruning jointly studies
SwiGLU pruning and cache-aware masks on resource-constrained hardware
~\cite{dip}. ARM-Sparse differs by holding hardware, mask budgets, and executor
comparisons fixed to measure where contiguous blocks cross both a quality gate
and a strong Apple CPU dense baseline.
