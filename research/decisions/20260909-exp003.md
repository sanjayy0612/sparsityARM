# EXP-003 implementation boundary

Date: 2026-09-09

Decision: implement the quality-measurement machinery before choosing or
running the real evaluation corpus.

Rationale:

- EXP-002 showed that expanding irregular neuron masks destroys the desired
  block sparsity, so the next uncertainty is whether direct block selection
  preserves useful model behavior.
- Selection by post-SwiGLU activation is an oracle/reference procedure. It can
  isolate the quality cost of blockification but cannot represent deployable
  end-to-end inference.
- A random-block negative control is required to show that the activation-aware
  ranking carries useful signal.
- Corpus revision, evaluation size, and the acceptable perplexity increase are
  consequential research choices. They remain subject to human approval and
  must be fixed before inspecting the real results.

Deferred: predictor design, sparse execution integration, performance claims,
task benchmarks, and alternative block-scoring functions.
