# P006 — PowerInfer-2: Fast Large Language Model Inference on a Smartphone

- **Authors:** Zhenliang Xue, Yixin Song, Zeyu Mi, Xinrui Zheng, Yubin Xia, Haibo Chen
- **Year:** 2024
- **Primary source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06282
- **Verification status:** verified from the authors' arXiv record

## Relevance

PowerInfer-2 uses neuron clusters as scheduling and storage units across mobile
CPU/NPU/memory resources. Consequently, grouping neurons is prior art.
ARM-Sparse's defensible contribution is the controlled measurement of the
quality–latency sparsity-granularity frontier on an Apple ARM CPU, not the
existence of neuron clusters.
